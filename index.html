About

About this site

This is a personal project that shares travel inspiration, theme park information and a visitor registration form.

Visitors can log their information, say how they found the page and leave comments for follow up. The form validates every field in plain JavaScript: required fields, a two letter state, a five digit zip, a phone number and an email address, each with its own message under the field.

How it is built

Three scripts with separate jobs: validation.js holds the validation library, page.js switches sections, and main.js wires everything up on DOMContentLoaded. No framework, no build step.
